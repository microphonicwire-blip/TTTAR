## Summary

The individual known as Undex engaged in a deliberate and sustained campaign of Child Pornographic Media spam across multiple Federal Gamers channels. Upon receiving reports from members, moderation staff intervened — at which point Undex escalated by redirecting the CP directly at moderators. The incident is currently under review pending assessment of further alternate account activity.

- Upon receiving reports, Undex chose to escalate rather than cease — directly targeting the moderators who were attempting to intervene
- The rapid creation of an alternate account following the ban demonstrates a level of premeditation inconsistent with a one-off outburst
- Taken alongside Undex's broader record, this incident further establishes a consistent pattern of deliberate provocation, disregard for server authority, and willingness to evade consequences through alternate accounts
