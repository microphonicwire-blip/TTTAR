# I012 – Grooming-Adjacent Messaging Behaviour

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Incident ID** | I012 |
| **Date** | 2025 |
| **Perpetrator** | [Undex](../people/undex) |
| **Severity** | 🔴 Critical |
| **Status** | Confirmed |

---

## Summary

Multiple younger members of Federal Gamers independently reported receiving unsolicited private messages from Undex that followed a consistent pattern: initial friendly conversation and gift offers (Robux, in-game items), followed by requests for personal information (age, location, photos), escalating to increasingly personal and inappropriate questions. At least one member reported receiving messages that were explicitly sexual in nature.

The pattern is consistent with recognised grooming behaviour.

---

## Affected Members

Three members reported this behaviour independently. All three were under 16. Their identities are withheld from this record.

---

## Timeline

| Time | Event |
|------|-------|
| Over several weeks | Undex initiates DM contact with multiple younger members |
| Week 3 | First affected member reports discomfort to a trusted mod |
| Week 3, Day 2 | Mod escalates to Joshua; investigation opened |
| Week 3, Day 4 | Two further members come forward independently after being told investigation was underway |
| Week 3, Day 5 | DM logs reviewed across all three cases; pattern confirmed |
| Week 3, Day 5 | Undex immediately and permanently blacklisted |

---

## Response

Undex was permanently blacklisted with immediate effect. All three affected members were supported directly. The matter was escalated beyond server moderation and reported to Discord Trust & Safety with full log documentation. Leadership issued a server-wide reminder about reporting uncomfortable DM interactions.

This incident is considered the most serious in Undex's logged history.

---

## Notes

> This record exists to document the behaviour pattern, not to expose affected members. No identifying information about those involved is stored here.
