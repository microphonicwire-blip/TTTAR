# I008 – Leaking of Private Voice Conversations

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Incident ID** | I008 |
| **Date** | 2025 |
| **Perpetrator** | [Undex](../people/undex) |
| **Severity** | 🔴 Critical |
| **Status** | Confirmed |

---

## Summary

During a period in which Undex held mod access, they were present in multiple private leadership voice channels. It was later discovered that Undex had been **recording these sessions** using OBS and selectively leaking edited clips to members of a rival server, **Fish Gang**, with added commentary intended to embarrass or discredit Federal Gamers leadership.

The recordings included candid conversations about internal server disputes, personal information shared in confidence by members, and strategic planning discussions.

---

## Timeline

| Time | Event |
|------|-------|
| Multiple sessions | Undex silently records private VC sessions over approximately 3 weeks |
| Week 4 | Clips begin circulating in Fish Gang's server |
| Week 4, Day 3 | A Fish Gang member leaks the clips back to FG leadership as a gesture |
| Week 4, Day 4 | Clips are traced back to Undex via audio fingerprinting and unique detail only Undex was present for |
| Week 4, Day 5 | Undex confronted; claims clips were "already public" |

---

## Impact

- Severe breach of trust affecting all members who participated in recorded sessions
- Personal details of at least two members discussed in recordings were exposed to a hostile audience
- Significant damage to server morale and willingness to use voice channels openly

---

## Response

Undex's mod role and VC access were revoked immediately. Affected members were informed. The incident contributed directly to Undex's permanent blacklist classification.
