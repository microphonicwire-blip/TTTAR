# Event E002 — The Fake Federal Gamers War

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Event ID** | E002 |
| **Date** | February 2024 — ~April 2024 |
| **Type** | Server Conflict / Impersonation |
| **Severity** | 🔴 High |
| **Status** | Resolved |

---

## Summary

Following a ban from Federal Gamers, Undex created a server called **"Federal Gamers"** — a direct impersonation of the real server, referred to here as **FFG (Fake Federal Gamers)**. Undex recruited his Spanish and Mexican contacts, manipulating them into believing FFG was the legitimate server. A two-month conflict followed before Audon successfully infiltrated and dismantled FFG from the inside.

---

## Timeline

| Step | What Happened |
|------|--------------|

| Infiltration | Audon uses this to delete all channels within FFG |

## Notes

- Undex's critical mistake was granting the Member role admin permissions, which Audon exploited
- Many of Undex's recruited contacts were unaware they had joined a fake server
- This event demonstrated a pattern of server impersonation and manipulation that remains a concern

---

## Outcome

FFG was successfully dismantled from the inside by Audon. The real Federal Gamers continued operating. Undex remained banned.
