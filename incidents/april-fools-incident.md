## Summary

Audon secretly orchestrated a National Socialist-themed server takeover during Federal Gamers' April Fools event, while deliberately planting evidence to frame Joshua Baloni (Oceanwave) and Undex as the responsible parties. The operation caused major community disruption, multiple bans, Doge's resignation as Co-Leader, and a mental health crisis for the original server owner.

---

## Background

Audon contacted **Moon** via DM to plan the April Fools operation, proposing an authoritarian takeover theme. Moon redesigned the server under a National Socialist aesthetic in cooperation with Audon — in place of Undex.

Audon then operated the **Fish Gang administrative account**, staging its activity to falsely implicate Undex as its operator. Throughout the operation, Audon performed ignorance of his own involvement while projecting distress.

{: .important }
Audon deliberately left Moon without exculpatory evidence, ensuring Moon could not expose his involvement.

The **Oceanwave** account was confirmed as belonging to Joshua Baloni. The Fish Gang Management account was operated by Audon, staged convincingly enough that the majority of members accepted the framing as genuine.

---

## FG v. Joshua Baloni

Doge moved to defend Baloni after planted evidence positioned him and Undex as the wrongdoers. After negotiations, Doge proposed a supervised reinstatement — an "ankle monitor" arrangement — which Audon eventually accepted.

Upon reinstatement, Baloni disclosed the full truth. Due to Audon's planted evidence, only **Doge** and **Undex** believed him. Baloni was then banned without stated cause — attributed to Audon — resulting in Doge resigning as Co-Leader.
