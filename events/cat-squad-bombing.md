# Event E001 — The Cat Squad Bombing

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Date** | March 2025 |
| **Type** | Server Raid / Conflict / Conspiracy |
| **Severity** | 🟡 Medium |
| **Status** | Resolved |

---

## Summary

Beginning around March 2025, Audon was invited to bring Federal Gamers members into **Cat Squad**, a server owned by Charlie (Fluffy). After growing to dislike the server's culture and developing a conflict with a member known as PoopCat, Audon and Josh coordinated a raid that resulted in the complete destruction of the Cat Squad server.

---

| Person | Role |

| Easy | Indirect cause; Audon cited Easy's behaviour change as motivation |

---

## Method

The raid was executed by socially engineering Cat Squad members into granting Audon admin privileges. Once obtained, Audon pinged `@everyone`, deleted every channel in front of the members, then left a single channel titled *"Bombed by Federal Gamers."*

The diss paragraphs Audon wrote against PoopCat were later found to be plagiarised from content creators **Packgod** and **Koofy**.

---

## Ongoing Concerns

{: .warning }
Following the raid, Audon created **FG Bureaucracy** — reportedly used to build cases against individuals and secretly store harvested IP addresses. The location of this server and its data remains unknown.

---

## Notes

- Josh showed hesitation during the raid but did not act to stop it
- Cat Squad had 3 backup servers prepared in advance
- Audon stated the raid was done *"for Easy"* and expressed no remorse
