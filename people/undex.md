## Summary

Undex met Joshua Baloni in a Roblox game called **Waste of Space (WOS)** and was welcomed into Federal Gamers. Despite being given multiple opportunities including mod and admin roles, Undex consistently abused trust and caused catastrophic damage to the server and its members. Known for presenting a friendly, helpful exterior while coordinating large-scale destruction and deeply inappropriate behaviour behind the scenes.

Undex is considered one of the most damaging individuals to have ever been granted access to Federal Gamers infrastructure. Their actions escalated from petty server trolling to targeted harassment, privacy violations, and the deliberate distribution of harmful content. Multiple second chances were extended and exploited in each case.

## Behaviour Profile

Undex demonstrates a consistent pattern of **trust exploitation** — building genuine relationships with server leadership before leveraging that access for destructive ends. Notably, Undex has shown no remorse across any logged incident and has on multiple occasions attempted to gaslight affected parties or shift blame onto other members.

Particularly concerning behaviours include:

- Recruiting outside actors to participate in attacks on the server
- Maintaining simultaneous membership in rival and hostile servers while holding mod roles
- Deliberately targeting younger or more vulnerable members for harassment
- Sending unsolicited explicit content to multiple members across DMs
- Attempting to obtain Discord account tokens through social engineering and fake bots
- Recording and leaking private voice channel conversations without consent
- Compiling and threatening to release personal information about members
